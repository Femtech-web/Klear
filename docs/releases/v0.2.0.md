**Let an AI trade your money without giving it the keys.** Your rules live in a NEAR contract with no keys, the only
signer of your accounts on Hyperliquid and Base. If the AI fails, anyone can bring the money home, and is paid only
once it arrives. Built at NEAR HACKATHON.

Site: https://klear-near.vercel.app · For judges: [JUDGES.md](https://github.com/Femtech-web/Klear/blob/main/JUDGES.md) · For AI agents: [AGENTS.md](https://github.com/Femtech-web/Klear/blob/main/AGENTS.md)

## New in v0.2.0: private, and still provable

- **Try to break it, live, with no wallet.** On the site's [`/try`](https://klear-near.vercel.app/try#break-it) page,
  each button sends a real testnet transaction from a stranger's account to a locked judge, and the judge refuses it
  on chain: trade with the investor's money (`mandate:not-the-manager`), pull the money out
  (`mandate:not-the-investor`), rewrite the rules (no such method; 0 access keys).
- **Sealed AI decisions, verified on NEAR.** The manager can decide inside a TEE on NEAR AI Cloud, its strategy
  encrypted end to end. Each decision's signed receipt goes on chain, and the locked `klear-tee` contract has NEAR's
  own DCAP verifier check the model's Intel TDX quote, then checks each receipt's signature. A sealed trade filled:
  buy 0.006 ETH, signed by the judge, receipt verified on NEAR
  ([`FQWtDbwk…`](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/FQWtDbwkHFMfBnxv5U8hA6M9bj19xdmGbWX6u4CFQc45)).
- **A confidential mandate on mainnet, with real funds.** A NEAR Intents account under the investor's mandate: a
  sealed AI traded inside it, a withdrawal to a stranger was refused (`policy_destination_denied`), and the exit sent
  everything home. Its owner key exists only in NEAR's MPC network, and a locked contract (`klear-owner`) is its only
  signer: after setup it signs only readable grant changes, on the investor's exit or the manager's silence.
- **An off-the-shelf agent rescues for pay.** Claude Code, unmodified, with only Klear's MCP tools and a faucet
  account, found strategies whose rescue was due, checked them, rescued them and was paid 0.15 NEAR each time, only
  once the money reached the investor. Every run is recorded with its full transcript, including one that failed
  ([docs/results/agent-runs](https://github.com/Femtech-web/Klear/tree/main/docs/results/agent-runs)).
- **A hosted MCP server**: `claude mcp add --transport http klear https://klear-near.vercel.app/api/mcp`
  (read-only: list, inspect, rescue status, how to rescue, verify a sealed receipt).
- **The site reads Klear live**: rescues paid on arrival and NEAR paid to rescuers, summed from every judge's own
  payouts; the seven contracts' keys and code; whether a rescue is open now. Grok 4's real Alpha Arena account, told
  twice: what the AI did ($10,000 to $325), and what a 30% floor would have done ($6,801 home).
- **Renamed from Keel to Klear.** Deployed, locked contracts keep their names (`keel-*` accounts, `KeelLeg`,
  `KeelVault`, `KeelDeployer`) and their source stays byte for byte, so the published hashes still match.

Fixes: the daily drill and the courier recover from a failed EVM send instead of crashing; a failed send can no
longer hide inside a script's output; the sealed manager relays an approved order within seconds; an agent can tell
when its rescue process has stopped and restart it. Every change: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/Femtech-web/Klear/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Check it in two minutes

```bash
curl -s https://test.rpc.fastnear.com -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"query","params":{"request_type":"view_access_key_list","finality":"final","account_id":"delever.keel-factory-bcf915.testnet"}}' \
  | python3 -c 'import json,sys;print(len(json.load(sys.stdin)["result"]["keys"]), "access keys")'
```

You should see `0 access keys`. Three more checks (the six guardian checks, who was paid and for what, the TEE
verified on NEAR) are in the README's [Try it](https://github.com/Femtech-web/Klear#try-it).

## Limits

Testnets with test funds, except two authorized mainnet privacy tests with a few dollars of the author's own. Not
audited. One market (an ETH perpetual on Hyperliquid, WETH on Base). The judges do not yet require a sealed receipt
before signing an order. A confidential mandate's ledger is access control, not cryptography, and its policy is
enforced by the provider's backend. The full list: the README's [Limits](https://github.com/Femtech-web/Klear#limits).
